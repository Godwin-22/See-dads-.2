FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing and services.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fees for Starter, Growth, or Pro plans. Enterprise clients may have a one-time onboarding fee depending on complexity.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, card payments, and Paystack. Enterprise clients can also pay via invoice with net-30 terms.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all plans. If you are not satisfied, contact us for a full refund.

How long are the contracts?

All plans are month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Annual plans receive a 20% discount.

Can I get a custom quote?

Absolutely. Contact our team for a tailored proposal based on your specific needs and goals.
